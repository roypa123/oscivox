This policy explains how the Oscivox app ("the app") handles information. The app is operated by ROY P A. We keep this policy short because the app collects very little.

01 Information we collect

Oscivox does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect, store or share your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, microphone recordings or any other personal information. The app does not use your microphone, camera, contacts or location.

Your settings (such as the frequency, volume and waveform you choose) are used only while the app is open and are not sent anywhere.

02 How the app uses your device

  • Audio: the app generates tones and plays them through your device's speaker or headphones. Nothing is recorded.
  • Internet: the app requests internet access to download its typefaces from Google Fonts the first time it runs, and to load advertisements (see section 3). When your device downloads a font, Google receives standard connection information such as your IP address, as with any web request. Google's handling of that information is described in its privacy policy.

03 Advertising

Oscivox is free and shows banner advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Ad networks may collect and use certain information from your device to show ads and measure their performance. This can include your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and approximate location derived from your IP address. We do not control this collection. It is governed by the ad network's own policies:

  • Google AdMob: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

04 Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. The only parties that receive any data are the third-party services named above (Google Fonts and the ad network), and only as described in this policy.

05 Data retention and deletion

Because the app does not collect personal information, we hold no personal data about you to delete. Uninstalling the app removes everything it stored on your device.

06 Children's privacy

Oscivox is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at the address below and we will help.

07 Security

The app does not transmit personal data, so there is none for us to protect. Network requests made by the app and its ad network use secure connections where available.

08 Hearing safety notice

Oscivox plays pure tones, which can be unpleasant or harmful at high volume. Start with a low volume, increase it slowly, and stop if anything sounds painful. The app is a sound and educational tool and is not a medical device.

09 Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
